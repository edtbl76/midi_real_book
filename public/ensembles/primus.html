Les Claypool

Les Claypool is the bass voice for the Primus pairing label. In the local ranking corpus, Les Claypool is treated as a virtuoso bassist because of idiosyncratic slap/tap/chordal technique and rhythmic independence. Their core style vocabulary is rock, alternative, avant-garde, funk; in this ensemble, that vocabulary is being aimed at avant-garde / punk-funk inside the broader Primus lane.
