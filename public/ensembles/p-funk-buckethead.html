Bootsy Collins

Bootsy Collins is the bass voice for the P-Funk / Buckethead pairing label. In the local ranking corpus, Bootsy Collins is treated as an everyone-else bassist because of deep funk placement, space, repetition, controlled variation. Their core style vocabulary is funk, R&B, soul; in this ensemble, that vocabulary is being aimed at funk inside the broader Parliament-Funkadelic lane.
