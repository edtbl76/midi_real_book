# Ensemble: modern fusion virtuoso

## Context

This ensemble collects some of the most technically formidable players in contemporary jazz fusion, connected most directly through the world of Chick Corea's Elektric Band and its legacy. Dave Weckl and Scott Kinsey both worked extensively within that orbit. Guthrie Govan represents the next generation of complete, technically limitless fusion guitarists via the Aristocrats. Hadrien Feraud emerged from the Paris jazz scene and John McLaughlin's 4th Dimension band as one of the most extraordinarily fast and musical bassists of his generation.

## Hadrien Feraud

Hadrien Feraud is a French bassist born in 1984 who emerged on the international jazz-fusion scene through his work with John McLaughlin's 4th Dimension band in the mid-2000s. His fingerstyle technique is almost without equal in terms of speed and clarity at high tempos, yet his playing remains musically sophisticated rather than purely demonstrative. Feraud's solo records demonstrate compositional maturity alongside the technical ability.

## Guthrie Govan

Guthrie Govan is a British guitarist widely considered one of the most technically complete guitarists active today. He is fluent in virtually every style — rock, blues, country, jazz, fusion, flamenco — and applies that breadth without sounding like a compilation of influences. He first came to wide attention through his work with Asia, subsequently through his fusion trio the Aristocrats, and through solo records that reveal an improvisational mind of unusual sophistication.

## Dave Weckl

Dave Weckl is one of the most influential drummers in contemporary jazz fusion, best known for his decade-long tenure with Chick Corea's Elektric Band from 1986 to 1994. His playing combines extraordinary technical precision — particularly his speed and control of the snare drum — with deep groove authority and sophisticated musical intelligence. Weckl's work with the Elektric Band established a template for technical jazz-fusion drumming that influenced an entire generation.

## Scott Kinsey

Scott Kinsey is a keyboard player best known as a core member of Tribal Tech, the jazz-fusion group led by bassist Gary Willis and guitarist Scott Henderson. Kinsey's playing synthesizes jazz harmonic sophistication with synthesizer texture and electric keyboards, functioning both as harmonic architect and colorist within the band's complex arrangements. He has also worked extensively as a solo artist and sideman in the Los Angeles jazz-fusion scene.
