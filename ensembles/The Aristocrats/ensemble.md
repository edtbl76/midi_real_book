# Ensemble: The Aristocrats

## Context

The Aristocrats formed in 2011 when Guthrie Govan (UK), Marco Minnemann (Germany), and Bryan Beller (USA) — all veterans of the session and touring world — decided to form a permanent trio. Their albums mix densely written prog-fusion heads with wide-open improvisation, and the band is known for balancing extreme technical demands with a dry, irreverent humor.

## Bryan Beller

Bryan Beller is a New York-based bassist whose career spans Steve Vai's touring band, Joe Satriani's G3 tours, and The Aristocrats. His playing in this trio is characterized by locked-in odd-meter grooves, melodic fills that don't clutter the guitar, and a clean modern tone that sits perfectly in a three-piece format with no keyboard to fill harmonic space.

## Guthrie Govan

Guthrie Govan is a British guitarist widely regarded as one of the most complete stylistic technicians working today, equally fluent in clean fingerstyle, hard rock, country, jazz, and funk. In The Aristocrats he is the primary melodic and harmonic voice, writing intricate compositions and improvising across them with a relaxed authority that belies their difficulty.

## Marco Minnemann

Marco Minnemann is a German drummer with an unusually large recorded output — dozens of solo albums alongside high-profile touring gigs with Joe Satriani and Steve Hackett. In The Aristocrats he is the rhythmic architect of some of the most metrically complex trio music in modern rock, yet his playing consistently serves the ensemble feel over personal display.
