# Ensemble: funk-fusion session

## Context

This ensemble overlaps almost entirely with the "abstract fusion" lineup — Gary Willis, Scott Henderson, Wayne Krantz, Dean Brown, Kirk Covington, and Scott Kinsey all circle the same Tribal Tech axis but here the focus shifts toward the funk-heavier, groove-centric side of their work. Tribal Tech's compositions ranged from through-composed jazz to raw funk, and this selection emphasizes the latter tendency: the way Willis's displaced bass lines, Covington's kick-drum authority, and Henderson's rhythm-guitar pocket create funk that is technically demanding without losing its body.

## Gary Willis

Gary Willis is a bassist associated with jazz fusion. His fingerstyle technique on fretted bass creates a fretless-adjacent quality — highly expressive articulation, rhythmic displacement, and harmonic control that goes well beyond standard root-fifth playing. He co-led Tribal Tech and has released solo albums extending his interest in odd meters and complex groove.

## Dean Brown

Dean Brown is a guitarist associated with jazz fusion, funk, and session work. His rhythm guitar pocket — built from formal jazz training and practical work with Bob Berg, Mike Stern, and Dave Weckl — reflects a discipline and precision that serves funk-fusion particularly well. His comping style is highly syncopated without sacrificing the underlying groove.

## Kirk Covington

Kirk Covington is a drummer associated with jazz fusion and funk. He was Tribal Tech's primary drummer, developing an approach that balanced the real-time interaction required by Henderson's improvisation with the groove-locking required by Willis's bass lines. His hi-hat patterns and kick placement are among the most studied elements of Tribal Tech's rhythm-section sound.

## Scott Kinsey

Scott Kinsey is a keyboard player associated with jazz fusion. His synthesizer textures — layering analog pads, electric piano, and synth lines — give Tribal Tech's funk-heavier tracks a dense harmonic environment that never competes with Henderson's guitar for melodic territory. He has recorded solo albums exploring electronic-influenced fusion.
