# Ensemble: Intervals

## Context

Intervals is a Canadian progressive metal band founded by guitarist Aaron Marshall. The band's sound sits at the intersection of djent-influenced progressive metal and jazz-fusion: 7-string guitars tuned low, complex polyrhythmic riffs, and clean-toned melodic sections that draw on jazz harmony. Plini is an Australian guitarist from the same progressive-fusion scene who has collaborated and toured with Intervals. Simon Grove and Troy Wright form the rhythm section. The band's evolution across albums moves from heavier djent toward increasingly melodic and production-polished progressive rock.

## Simon Grove

Simon Grove is the bassist of Intervals and a member of the Australian progressive-rock/fusion scene. His bass work supports Aaron Marshall's complex riff architecture while contributing melodic lines in the band's cleaner sections. He also plays with Plini's live band, making him central to both halves of this ensemble's extended circle.

## Plini

Plini (b. 1991, Sydney) is an Australian guitarist, composer, and producer who releases music independently and has become a central figure in the modern progressive fusion scene. His playing is characterized by legato fluency, harmonic sophistication, and an unusually clean tone for the genre. He shares the progressive-fusion scene with Intervals and has toured with them. His albums — *Handmade Cities* (2016), *Sunhead* (2018), *Impulse Voices* (2022) — define the melodic prog-fusion aesthetic.

## Aaron Marshall

Aaron Marshall is the founding guitarist and primary composer of Intervals. His 7-string guitar work blends djent-style low-register rhythmic riffing with jazz-influenced clean-tone melodic playing. He formed Intervals in Toronto in 2011 after earlier band experiences in the progressive metal scene. His compositional approach has evolved from heavier, more riff-centric early work toward melodic progressive rock with jazz harmony.

## Troy Wright

Troy Wright is the drummer of Intervals and has been the band's primary drummer through their key albums. He plays in the modern progressive metal tradition: precise execution of complex polyrhythms, heavy kick patterns that lock with the guitar's low-register riffs, and the dynamic range to move from blast-adjacent heaviness to jazz-lite sensitivity in the band's clean sections.
