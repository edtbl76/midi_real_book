# Ensemble: Plini

## Context

Plini (Oliver Messiaen-influenced stage name adopted by Oliver Pulvirenti) is a Sydney-based guitarist and composer who self-released a series of EPs from 2012 before his 2016 full-length debut "Handmade Cities" established him as a leading voice in progressive instrumental guitar. His music combines djent-derived rhythmic precision with jazz harmony, legato melodic phrasing, and an unusually warm and transparent guitar tone; "Impulse Voices" (2022) deepened the compositional ambition further. Collaborators including Simon Grove, Aaron Marshall, and Troy Wright are central to how the recorded work sounds.

## Plini

Plini (b. 1992, Sydney, Australia) is a self-taught guitarist and composer who built an online audience through self-released EPs before signing to serjical strike Records for "Handmade Cities" (2016). His playing voice centers on a fluid legato technique, jazz-influenced harmonic thinking, and a clean-to-light-crunch tone that foregrounds note clarity over saturation. He produces his own records and handles all guitar and most compositional work on his releases.

## Aaron Marshall

Aaron Marshall is the founder and primary guitarist of Intervals, a Canadian progressive metal band known for its melodic approach to djent and its clean guitar tones. He has appeared as a guest collaborator on Plini's recordings, contributing his own legato and rhythmic approach to the ensemble sound.

## Troy Wright

Troy Wright is the drummer on Plini's studio recordings, bringing a jazz-informed approach to a progressive metal context — his time feel, dynamic gradation, and brush-to-stick range give the music a flexibility that heavier djent drumming typically lacks.
