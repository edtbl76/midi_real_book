# Ensemble: Mahavishnu / Shakti

## Context

The Mahavishnu Orchestra and Shakti represent John McLaughlin's two landmark ensemble voices of the 1970s: the former drove jazz-rock fusion to its most intense and technically demanding extreme, while the latter stripped away amplification entirely to explore the meeting point between South Indian classical music and jazz improvisation. Both groups demanded a level of rhythmic and harmonic mastery drawn from non-Western traditions that was virtually unprecedented in Western popular music. The players in this file span both worlds, from the electric ferocity of the Mahavishnu Orchestra to the acoustic intimacy of Shakti and its 1990s reunion under the name Remember Shakti.

## Dominique Di Piazza

Dominique Di Piazza is a French bassist known for an unusually fast three-finger plucking technique (ring–middle–index) that allows him to generate melodic lines at speeds more associated with guitar than bass. His fretless tone is warm and vocal, and his command of chromatic harmony lets him navigate the complex modulations of McLaughlin's writing without losing the groove.

## John McLaughlin

John McLaughlin is a guitarist whose career encompasses Miles Davis's electric period, the jazz-rock hurricane of the Mahavishnu Orchestra, the acoustic Indian-classical fusion of Shakti, and decades of trio and solo work. His defining traits are flat-pick velocity in odd meters, a command of both Western modal harmony and Indian raga vocabulary, and the ability to shift between devastating electric intensity and delicate acoustic lyricism within a single body of work.

## Trilok Gurtu

Trilok Gurtu is an Indian percussionist who synthesizes tabla technique, jazz drum-set thinking, and a vast vocabulary of hand percussion into a single, unified approach. He is equally comfortable as a colorist in a chamber setting and as the rhythmic engine of a jazz trio. His work with McLaughlin in the early 1990s is the primary context for this ensemble file.

## L. Shankar

L. Shankar is an Indian violinist who was a founding member of Shakti and one of the first players to bring Carnatic violin technique into a sustained cross-cultural ensemble context. He plays a custom double-neck electric violin of his own design, giving him a tonal range unavailable on a standard instrument. His Shakti recordings are his most widely heard work, though he has also collaborated with Peter Gabriel and co-founded the WOMAD festival.

## Zakir Hussain

Zakir Hussain (1951–2024) was the pre-eminent tabla player of his generation, a defining collaborator with both Shakti and its 1999 reunion as Remember Shakti, and a tireless cross-cultural musician who worked with Yo-Yo Ma, Mickey Hart, and many others. His tabla playing combined classical North Indian virtuosity with an acute sensitivity to jazz improvisation, making him uniquely suited to McLaughlin's ensemble contexts.
