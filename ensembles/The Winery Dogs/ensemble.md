# Ensemble: The Winery Dogs

## Context

The Winery Dogs formed in 2013 as a hard rock power trio built around Richie Kotzen (guitar and lead vocals), Billy Sheehan (bass), and Mike Portnoy (drums), with the explicit goal of capturing an organic, live-sounding energy rather than the tightly edited sheen of mainstream studio metal. What distinguishes their interplay is the convergence of three distinctive voices: Kotzen's hybrid-picking technique and blues-soul vocal phrasing, Sheehan's lead-bass countermelodies that weave above and around the guitar, and Portnoy's powerful groove drumming anchored in rock feel but decorated with a prog-rock fill vocabulary.

## Marco Mendoza

Marco Mendoza is a Mexican-American bassist known for his work in hard rock and melodic rock contexts. He came to prominence through stints with Thin Lizzy and Whitesnake, where he developed a reputation for a fluid, melodic approach to bass playing that sits comfortably in the rock tradition while drawing on funk and Latin influences. His tone is warm and rounded, favoring a supportive role that locks in with the kick drum while adding melodic embellishments in open spaces. Mendoza's playing with Whitesnake and the Dead Daisies demonstrates his ability to anchor a band with consistent groove while contributing memorable bass hooks that lift the arrangement.

## Richie Kotzen

Richie Kotzen is a guitarist, vocalist, and songwriter who has built one of the most distinctive voices in hard rock and blues-rock over three decades. He rose to early attention as a solo artist on the Shrapnel Records fusion-rock circuit before joining Poison in 1991, contributing to their "Native Tongue" album, and later co-founding Mr. Big's replacement lineup before departing for a prolific solo career. Kotzen's guitar playing is defined by his hybrid-picking technique — using a pick and fingers simultaneously — which gives his lines a fluid, legato quality that blends seamlessly with the blues and soul phrasing of his vocals. As a singer, his voice carries a genuine R&B and soul quality rare in hard rock, allowing him to deliver emotionally direct performances that set The Winery Dogs apart from more conventional metal trios. His solo catalog, spanning albums like "Mother Head's Family Reunion" (1994) and "Fever Dream" (2019), showcases a consistently evolving artist comfortable moving between blues, funk, and rock.

## Tommy Aldridge

Tommy Aldridge is an American drummer who has been a defining force in hard rock drumming since the 1970s, known above all for his technical power, his showmanship (including bare-handed drumming passages), and his ability to drive a band with enormous physical authority. He built his reputation through work with Pat Travers and Ozzy Osbourne before joining Whitesnake for extended periods, where his drumming contributed to some of the most commercially successful hard rock records of the 1980s. Aldridge plays with a forward-leaning attack that pushes the tempo without losing control, and his fills are dramatic and space-aware, designed to set up rather than overwhelm the next section. His double bass drum work and commanding snare are hallmarks that influenced a generation of rock drummers.
