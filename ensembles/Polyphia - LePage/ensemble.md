# Ensemble: Polyphia

## Context

Polyphia formed in Dallas, TX in 2010 and developed a guitar-driven sound that blends math rock precision, trap and hip-hop rhythmic sensibility, clean neo-soul guitar tones, and video-game music influences into a style with no close precedent in metal or prog. The band operates without a permanent bassist on most recordings, using programmed bass and guest players; the guitar interplay between Tim Henson and Scott LePage is the structural center of all four albums — "Muse" (2014), "Renaissance" (2016), "New Levels New Devils" (2018), and "Remember That You Will Die" (2022).

## Clay Gober

Clay Gober is Polyphia's bassist and a co-founder of the band. He provides the low-end foundation on live performances and selected recordings, navigating the syncopated and math-influenced rhythmic language of the band's guitar parts with a precision-oriented, articulate approach.

## Scott LePage

Scott LePage is Polyphia's co-guitarist and the player whose harmonic and rhythmic contributions complement Henson's lead voice. Where Henson carries the primary melodic lines, LePage's guitar role shifts between rhythmic punctuation, harmonic color, and counter-melody depending on the track. He left the band after the "Remember That You Will Die" album cycle.

## Clay Aeschliman

Clay Aeschliman is a co-founder of Polyphia and the drummer on all four studio albums through "Remember That You Will Die" (2022). His playing integrates the trap and hip-hop rhythmic language that defines the band's feel — he is not a jazz or prog drummer applying independence to complex meters, but a player who brings the pocket and syncopation of contemporary hip-hop production into a live drum context. He departed following the "Remember That You Will Die" album cycle.
