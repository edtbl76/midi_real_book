# Ensemble: metal shred

## Context

This ensemble gathers players from the Yngwie Malmsteen orbit and the Swedish/European neoclassical metal tradition of the 1980s and 1990s. Malmsteen's Rising Force records established the template: Baroque-influenced harmonic minor scales, sweep arpeggios, classical-guitar conservatory technique applied to a Marshall-and-distortion context, and a rhythm section built from Swedish hard rock. Marcel Jacob was Malmsteen's primary bass collaborator on several Rising Force albums. Ian Haugland was the drummer for Europe, whose records occupy the same European hard rock space. Jens Johansson — brother of bassist Anders Johansson — brought jazz training and classical keyboard vocabulary to the Rising Force band. Vinnie Moore and Joe Stump each developed independent neoclassical-shred vocabularies in the American Shrapnel Records tradition.

## Marcel Jacob

Marcel Jacob is a bassist associated with hard rock and neoclassical metal. He was Malmsteen's bassist on *Odyssey* and *Eclipse* and the bass player for Swedish hard rock band Talisman, which he co-founded with Jeff Scott Soto. His playing in a metal context emphasized clarity, speed, and a melodic fill vocabulary that complemented — rather than just supported — the guitar lead lines. He died in 2009.

## Yngwie Malmsteen

Yngwie Malmsteen is a guitarist associated with neoclassical metal. He arrived in the United States from Sweden in 1983, and his playing on the Steeler and Alcatrazz albums immediately distinguished itself through its combination of classical-guitar training (Paganini, Bach), violin-influenced tremolo picking, and absolute command of the harmonic minor scale over distorted guitar. His solo debut *Rising Force* (1984) established the neoclassical metal genre.

## Vinnie Moore

Vinnie Moore is a guitarist associated with neoclassical metal and rock. He was signed to Shrapnel Records and released *Mind's Eye* in 1986, a neoclassical-shred album built on modal and harmonic minor vocabulary with a more melodic and spacious approach than Malmsteen's. He later joined UFO as a touring and recording guitarist, adapting his technique to a classic British hard rock context.

## Joe Stump

Joe Stump is a guitarist associated with neoclassical metal. He is a long-serving professor at Berklee College of Music and has released numerous solo albums through his own label and Shrapnel, maintaining a consistently high level of neoclassical shred output across three decades. His technique is notable for extreme alternate picking speed and Baroque-influenced sweep arpeggios.

## Ian Haugland

Ian Haugland is a drummer associated with hard rock. He has been the drummer for Europe since 1983 and appeared on all of the band's studio albums, from *Wings of Tomorrow* through the band's 2000s reunion and subsequent recordings. His playing is built on a driving hard rock style that serves big-riff anthemic song structures without overcomplicating them.

## Jens Johansson

Jens Johansson is a keyboard player associated with jazz, hard rock, and neoclassical metal. He is the brother of bassist Anders Johansson, and both were members of Yngwie Malmsteen's Rising Force band in the mid-1980s. Jens's jazz training — he studied piano and composition — gave the Rising Force records an unusual harmonic sophistication, and his solo work extends into bebop and progressive jazz directions that diverge sharply from the metal context.
