# Ensemble: Planet X

## Context

Planet X is a fusion supergroup founded around 1999 by Derek Sherinian and Virgil Donati, built around an unusually high concentration of technical virtuosity — Donati's polyrhythmic independence, Sherinian's rock-weighted keyboard vocabulary, and a rotating cast of elite guitarists and bassists. The band's albums move between composed prog-fusion structures and open improvisational passages, often at extreme tempos and in odd meters. Their 2007 album *Quantum* marks a new lineup with Ric Fierabracci on bass and Brett Garsed on guitar, and represents the most documented version of this ensemble's four-way chemistry.

## Ric Fierabracci

Ric Fierabracci is a Los Angeles-based fusion bassist known for his melodic sensibility, clean articulation, and command of odd meters. He spent years in the Chick Corea Elektric Band II, one of the most demanding ensemble contexts in jazz-fusion, before joining Planet X for the *Quantum* album (2007).

## Brett Garsed

Brett Garsed is an Australian guitarist whose playing is deeply indebted to Allan Holdsworth — seamless legato, wide intervallic vocabulary, and a singing sustain that blurs the line between guitar and synthesizer. He joined Planet X for *MoonBabies* (2002) and remained through *Quantum* (2007), and is also known for his collaboration with T.J. Helmerich.

## Virgil Donati

Virgil Donati is an Australian drummer widely regarded as one of the most technically advanced drummers in the world. His playing combines extraordinary polyrhythmic independence — different meters and subdivisions simultaneously in each limb — with rock-level power and intensity. He is a founding member of Planet X and its rhythmic anchor across all four studio albums.

## Derek Sherinian

Derek Sherinian is an American keyboardist who served as Dream Theater's primary keyboardist from 1994 to 1999, replacing Kevin Moore. After leaving Dream Theater he co-founded Planet X with Virgil Donati. His keyboard style is rock-forward — heavily layered Hammond organ, aggressive Moog synthesis, and a voicing vocabulary built for power rather than delicacy. He is also a member of Black Country Communion.
