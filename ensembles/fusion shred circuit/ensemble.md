# Ensemble: fusion shred circuit

## Context

This ensemble connects the CAB project — Bunny Brunel and Tony MacAlpine's fusion group with Dennis Chambers — to the broader jazz fusion world of Chick Corea and the neoclassical shred tradition that MacAlpine and Jon Finn inhabit. CAB recorded three albums (2000–2006) that combined Chambers's formidable drumming with MacAlpine's classical-piano-trained guitar work in a fusion context that was more technically demanding than most of its contemporaries. Chick Corea's Elektric Band provides the harmonic sophistication axis, and Jon Finn contributes a New England conservatory-based neoclassical shred vocabulary.

## Bunny Brunel

Bunny Brunel is a bassist associated with jazz fusion. He worked extensively with Chick Corea in the early 1980s and later co-founded CAB with Tony MacAlpine. His electric bass lines have a melodic, liquid quality built on jazz vocabulary applied to a rock-fusion context. He was born in France and developed his approach partly in the Nice conservatory before moving to the United States.

## Tony MacAlpine

Tony MacAlpine is a guitarist associated with neoclassical metal and jazz fusion. He trained as a classical pianist before taking up guitar, and this foundation gives his phrasing — particularly his use of diminished and harmonic minor scales — a compositional logic that distinguishes him from pure technique-focused shred guitarists. He has recorded extensively for Shrapnel and Mascot, and co-led CAB.

## Jon Finn

Jon Finn is a guitarist associated with neoclassical rock and jazz fusion. He founded the Jon Finn Group and has taught extensively at Berklee College of Music. His compositions blend jazz harmony with neoclassical guitar technique, and his published studies — including *Advanced Modern Rock Guitar Improvisations* — are widely used teaching materials. He is less commercially prominent than MacAlpine but equally serious as a practitioner.

## Dennis Chambers

Dennis Chambers is a drummer associated with jazz fusion and funk. He is one of the most technically complete drummers of his generation, with an extraordinary ability to play very complex patterns at high tempos while maintaining a deep groove. He has worked with Parliament-Funkadelic, John Scofield, Mike Stern, Santana, CAB, and many others.

## Chick Corea

Chick Corea is a keyboard player associated with jazz, jazz fusion, and avant-garde. He led Return to Forever — one of the definitive fusion bands of the 1970s — and then the Elektric Band in the 1980s, which was arguably even more technically demanding. His compositions are central to the jazz-fusion repertoire, and his piano and synthesizer approach shaped the harmonic language of the genre.
