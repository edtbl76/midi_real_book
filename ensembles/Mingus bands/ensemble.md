## Charles Mingus

Charles Mingus is the bass voice for the `Mingus bands` pairing label. In the local ranking corpus, Charles Mingus is treated as an everyone-else bassist because of aggressive walking lines, blues/gospel harmony, counterpoint, pedal figures, compositional bass authority. Their core style vocabulary is modern jazz, hard bop, blues, gospel-influenced jazz; in this ensemble, that vocabulary is being aimed at modern jazz / hard bop / blues inside the broader Mingus bands lane.
