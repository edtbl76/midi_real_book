# Ensemble: Peter Gabriel

## Context

Peter Gabriel's solo world is built on art rock, world music, and studio experimentation — a post-Genesis vision where texture, space, and electronics matter as much as melody. His long-running band (Tony Levin on bass, David Rhodes on guitar, Manu Katché or Jerry Marotta on drums) developed a signature sound across albums from *Melt* (1980) through *Up* (2002). The orbit extends into progressive rock through shared players with King Crimson and the broader art-rock scene that Belew, Collins, and others inhabited.

## Tony Levin

Tony Levin is Peter Gabriel's primary bassist since 1977, playing on virtually every Gabriel solo album. A cornerstone of Gabriel's sound, Levin brings Chapman Stick, fretless and fretted bass, and his own invention — Funk Fingers (drumstick extensions worn on two fingers for percussive attack). His work combines deep pocket playing with odd-meter precision and an extraordinary sensitivity to song-serving dynamics.

## David Rhodes

David Rhodes is Peter Gabriel's primary touring and studio guitarist since 1979. An understated, textural player, Rhodes specializes in atmospheric rhythm guitar, treated tones, and song-serving color rather than lead flash. He appears on *Melt* (1980), *Security* (1982), *So* (1986), *Us* (1992), and *Up* (2002), making him one of the most consistent voices in Gabriel's recorded catalog.

## Jordan Rudess

Jordan Rudess is the keyboardist of Dream Theater since 1999 and a technically prodigious player with conservatory training (Juilliard). A former member of the Dixie Dregs, he replaced Derek Sherinian in Dream Theater and has been a cornerstone of the band's sound ever since. He is an orbit player in this ensemble — not a Peter Gabriel collaborator — but his work with Levin and Petrucci in Liquid Tension Experiment places him in the same progressive rock space.

## Mel Collins

Mel Collins is a British saxophonist and flautist best known for his work with King Crimson on *In the Wake of Poseidon* (1970), *Lizard* (1970), *Islands* (1971), and *Earthbound* (1972). A sought-after session player, he also worked extensively with the Rolling Stones, Paul McCartney, and many others. He is an orbit player in this ensemble — connected to the progressive rock world through shared King Crimson lineage with Belew and Mastelotto.
