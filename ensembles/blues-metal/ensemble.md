# Ensemble: blues-metal

## Context

This ensemble draws from the European hard rock and neoclassical metal tradition of the late 1970s and early 1980s, centered on the bands orbiting Ozzy Osbourne and Rainbow. Randy Rhoads brought a classical guitar conservatory background to metal, fundamentally changing what "technique" meant in a rock context, while Ritchie Blackmore's baroque-influenced phrasing and Uli Jon Roth's proto-neoclassical vibrato independently developed related ideas. Gary Moore and Bob Daisley were frequent collaborators across both the Ozzy and post-Rainbow orbits, and Don Airey's keyboard orchestration supplied the harmonic architecture that gave these bands their cinematic weight.

## Bob Daisley

Bob Daisley is a bassist associated with hard rock and heavy metal. He was the primary bassist and frequent lyrical collaborator on Ozzy Osbourne's first two solo albums, working directly alongside Randy Rhoads to establish the rhythmic foundation beneath some of the most influential metal records of the era. He also recorded with Rainbow and Gary Moore.

## Gary Moore

Gary Moore is a guitarist associated with blues-rock and hard rock. He is distinctive for a career that moved between hard rock with Thin Lizzy and neoclassical-influenced metal (Colosseum II, solo records) before pivoting decisively to blues in the late 1980s. His vibrato is widely cited as one of the strongest in any genre. He died in 2011.

## Tommy Aldridge

Tommy Aldridge is a drummer associated with hard rock and heavy metal. He played with Ozzy Osbourne during the Randy Rhoads era on the *Diary of a Madman* tour and on *Speak of the Devil*, and later with Whitesnake and Zakk Wylde's Black Label Society. He is particularly known for his powerful bare-hands drumming technique — he frequently plays without sticks for extended passages.

## Don Airey

Don Airey is a keyboard player associated with hard rock and heavy metal. He is one of the most widely recorded keyboardists in the genre, having worked with Rainbow (appearing on *Rainbow Rising* and *Long Live Rock 'n' Roll*), Ozzy Osbourne (*Blizzard of Ozz* and *Diary of a Madman*), Gary Moore, Whitesnake, and eventually joining Deep Purple as a permanent member after Jon Lord's departure in 2002.
