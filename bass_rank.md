# Bassist Rank

VIRT: 44, EVERYONE ELSE: 45

Ranking basis: split the 86 bass players into two groups. `Virtuosos` means advanced electric-bass command: technique, articulation, speed/cleanliness, harmonic/rhythmic sophistication, improvisational range, extended techniques, or unusually difficult idiomatic vocabulary. `Everyone Else` can still include great, important, influential, or highly musical players; they are just not classified here as bass virtuosos.
