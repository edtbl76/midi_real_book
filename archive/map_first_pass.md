# First-Pass Bass to Guitar Map

Mapping rule: `IDIOMATIC-EXCELLENT-FIT` means the guitarist is a high-confidence fit for the bassist's core musical language. A fit must be more than genre-adjacent: the guitarist's vocabulary, feel, tone concept, rhythm/lead role, and broader body of work should plausibly support an idiomatic duet with that bassist.

Bandmates have a natural pull when the musical evidence supports it, but direct band association is neither required nor sufficient. Rows may have zero, one, or many guitarists. Empty rows are acceptable. Within each row, accepted guitarists are ordered by the current `guitar_rank.md` technical-virtuosity order.
